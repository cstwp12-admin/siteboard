# What changed
- **v0.6.2** – new **Map** tab: load the project KMZ/KML once (kept in the browser, not uploaded) and see the alignment, chainage marks, points of interest and ROW boundary on an OpenStreetMap or satellite basemap. Inspections and photos are plotted by GPS when the report carries it, otherwise by their chainage along the alignment; filter by period, category and person, click a pin for the photos. *Snap map* saves the view as image or PDF, sends it to WhatsApp with a description, or attaches it to a WhatsApp message or an internal memo. Fixed: photos of *no activity* items were not found (their photo card has no Status line).
- **v0.6.1** – memo numbers now follow a prefix / year / running number, default `SB/INT/MEMO/2026/001`; the prefix is set in Settings → Memo numbering and the running number continues automatically. *New memo* button in Messages & memo. Wording changed from “missing” to “no report”; a red ✕ can be marked *no report needed* (office work, leave, off site…) so it is not counted against compliance, and is shared through the settings file. Share / save the submission matrix and overview as image or PDF, or send to WhatsApp with an editable description. Guide and flow updated: the report admin is the RE or an assigned ARE and reports are kept in a folder on that person's computer (shared Drive folder planned for later).
- **v0.6.0** (previous release v0.5) – reports are built directly as PDF (file name, margins, page numbers, full-resolution photos) and as Word; photos are read at their original resolution and show a retry placeholder if one cannot load; *Open PDF* from the folder; WhatsApp comments / follow-ups with attachments; internal memo designer; Brave guidance; colours and fonts aligned with the SDR app; version shown in the app, reports and docs; MIT licence and third-party notices added.
- **Phase 4–5** – sidebar layout, Today view, phone menu, report logo, QR codes and links to the SDR app, SDR activity list, description-based structure matching with actual-chainage override and manual assignment.
- **Phase 3** – photo gallery, structure board, thumbnail cache.
- **Phase 2** – Excel export, missing-report message, reporting rules, observation follow-up, search.
- **Phase 1** – folder reading, matrices, weekly/monthly reports, Tidy folder, shared settings.
