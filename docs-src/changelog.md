# What changed
- **v0.10.0** – mobile and resilience: larger phone touch targets, responsive header/forms/map, full JSON backups of all workspace settings and registers, previewed restore into separate workspaces with rollback on storage failure, and an installable offline app shell. Source reports, settings files and map tiles are excluded from shell caching. Updates wait for an explicit reload. Memo counters are preserved; use one authoritative issuing workspace.
- **v0.9.0** – selected evidence sharing and focused reports. New **Share & focused report** view with a common item-level scope: dates, teams, reporters, categories, zones, chainage, side, structures, observations and text. Choose statistics sections, caption fields and photo order. Gallery selections stay selected when hidden by filters and can be shared as original photos with numbered captions, captioned cards or SDR-style table sheets. Long captions continue on labelled pages; PDF and Word focused reports use the same records. Structure and map shortcuts prefill their scope. Matrix/overview sharing can select dates and personnel independently; work filters do not distort compliance. Exports retain source references, and cancelled shares are not logged as sent. Photo batches are limited to 20 with an explicit message. Production uploads are made from tested versioned packages.
- **v0.8.2** – fixed map-statistics text clipping: reporter names, structure lists and headings wrap to measured widths, and the card grows to fit all selected categories and names. Tall infographic PDFs keep their aspect ratio and paginate when needed. Overview captions use the active project's alignment length. Sharing a period longer than 62 days retains the complete period: submission matrices are split into labelled date pages, with all pages included in previews, image downloads, WhatsApp attachments and PDF export. The full description is preserved in the PDF. Added statistics layout and 120-day export regression tests.
- **v0.8.1** – project-ready configuration: importing an SDR `setup.json` now updates the project chainage limits, zones, sections, centreline, structures and activity mappings together. Existing workspaces continue with the packaged WP12 defaults, old saved settings remain valid, and zones are derived from the active project without rewriting cached reports. The map uses the setup centreline automatically and keeps KMZ/KML as an optional source for ROW and points of interest. Setup and KMZ validation now give safer recovery messages. Added repeatable project, KMZ, all-view and real-SDR-report regression tests.
- **v0.8.0** – Map v2: select a chainage range by typing it or dragging along the alignment; generate and share a map-statistics infographic; show 100 m coverage; flag GPS points more than 250 m from their stated chainage; find a chainage or structure; export filtered pins as KML or GeoJSON; and share the submission matrix together with the current map. KMZ storage is now separated by workspace, and valid GPS points near the equator are no longer rejected.
- **v0.7.0** – workspaces and profile: each reports folder is a workspace with its own team, project code, memo numbering and register, and you can switch between them (for example CST and WPC). A profile (name and position, no passwords) sets the memo sender and shows administrator tools to RE, ARE, SOR and PM only. Memo numbers follow a template per workspace, default `SB/{TEAM}/{ROLE}/MEMO/{YEAR}/{NNN}`, and continue from the register. New **Issued register** for memos, messages, reports and shared pictures; files are saved in `_SiteBoard/Issued/<year>` in the reports folder, and the list is shared through the settings file, with a session log. The last tab and period are remembered. Submission matrix picture: the legend no longer runs off the edge.
- **v0.6.3** – fixed: reports with no-activity photos showed a false parsing warning (the photo page was read as an extra activity row); report and photo windows opened from the map now appear above the map.
- **v0.6.2** – new **Map** tab: load the project KMZ/KML once (kept in the browser, not uploaded) and see the alignment, chainage marks, points of interest and ROW boundary on an OpenStreetMap or satellite basemap. Inspections and photos are plotted by GPS when the report carries it, otherwise by their chainage along the alignment; filter by period, category and person, click a pin for the photos. *Snap map* saves the view as image or PDF, sends it to WhatsApp with a description, or attaches it to a WhatsApp message or an internal memo. Fixed: photos of *no activity* items were not found (their photo card has no Status line).
- **v0.6.1** – memo numbers now follow a prefix / year / running number, default `SB/INT/MEMO/2026/001`; the prefix is set in Settings → Memo numbering and the running number continues automatically. *New memo* button in Messages & memo. Wording changed from “missing” to “no report”; a red ✕ can be marked *no report needed* (office work, leave, off site…) so it is not counted against compliance, and is shared through the settings file. Share / save the submission matrix and overview as image or PDF, or send to WhatsApp with an editable description. Guide and flow updated: the report admin is the RE or an assigned ARE and reports are kept in a folder on that person's computer (shared Drive folder planned for later).
- **v0.6.0** (previous release v0.5) – reports are built directly as PDF (file name, margins, page numbers, full-resolution photos) and as Word; photos are read at their original resolution and show a retry placeholder if one cannot load; *Open PDF* from the folder; WhatsApp comments / follow-ups with attachments; internal memo designer; Brave guidance; colours and fonts aligned with the SDR app; version shown in the app, reports and docs; MIT licence and third-party notices added.
- **Phase 4–5** – sidebar layout, Today view, phone menu, report logo, QR codes and links to the SDR app, SDR activity list, description-based structure matching with actual-chainage override and manual assignment.
- **Phase 3** – photo gallery, structure board, thumbnail cache.
- **Phase 2** – Excel export, missing-report message, reporting rules, observation follow-up, search.
- **Phase 1** – folder reading, matrices, weekly/monthly reports, Tidy folder, shared settings.
